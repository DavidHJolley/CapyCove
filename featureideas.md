# Capy Cove feature ideas

Recorded September 6, 2026. These are proposals based on the code, not findings from a live playtest.

The goal is to make each visit feel purposeful, social, and memorable. Build on the existing 11 public rooms, 11 minigames, housing, quests, and events.

## 1. Give new players a tiny adventure

Build a story around the existing six-step guided tour. Help a beach capy find something, finish a short fishing round, earn a starter hat, and place a souvenir in a house. Aim for a satisfying first five minutes that teaches through doing.

## 2. Make playing together effortless

Extend population indicators, friend presence, and lobby notices with a “Play together” action. Let players join a friend's activity, keep their group together after a game, and vote on what to do next. Measure how often waiting players actually reach a match.

## 3. Give the island a shared project

For example, everyone contributes fruit to prepare a beach picnic. Contributions visibly add decorations, and completion opens a small celebration. Solo players can contribute too, giving separate activities a common purpose.

## 4. Make houses worth visiting

Add owner-selected activities, preset guestbook stamps, and housewarming invitations. Connect achievements to decorating with fishing trophies, kart podium souvenirs, and other keepsakes.

## 5. Create one memorable surprise per room

Examples include a secret revealed at sunset, capybaras gathering under shelter when it rains, and a musical prop that responds when friends join in. Small discoveries can give the world more personality.

## 6. Polish the things players touch constantly

Playtest movement, camera behavior, interaction targeting, game entry, and rematches. These are areas to investigate, not verified problems.

## Recommended first step

Run a five-player playtest, then make one focused update to the first five minutes. Watch where players hesitate, whether they finish a game, and whether they find another person to play with. Use those observations to choose the next feature.
