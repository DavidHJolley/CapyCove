What you get

A whole island, and nothing urgent to do on it

Capy Cove is built entirely from scratch: every model, texture and sound is generated in the browser, so there is nothing to install and nothing to wait for.

Ten minigames, and most of them want company

Race two rivals down Summit Sprint, hide as a barrel in the Glimmer Mine's Prop Hunt, guess doodles in the jungle, then take a seat in the Game Den for pong, shuffleboard, chess, four-in-a-row or the Capy Clash card duel. Reel Rush and Smoothie Scramble are yours alone, but everyone can watch.

A den of your own, furnished your way

Every capy gets a den. Twenty pieces of furniture in the shop, up to sixty of them placed wherever you like with a drag-and-drop editor, and a front door you can set to open, friends-only or invite-only.

An island that keeps living while you are away

One cove day is forty real minutes, and it runs from dawn to midnight for everybody at the same time. The sky drifts between clear, breezy and rain, snow falls on Frosty Peak, and once a day the aurora comes out over the mountain. If you miss it, you miss it.

Stamps and dailies you cannot buy your way past

Three daily quests, drawn from a pool of twenty-eight, plus a fifty-stamp album for the things worth doing once: riding the whale, winning a photo finish, striking a gem. Hit five, twelve, twenty, thirty and forty stamps and the cove hands over a snorkel, an aurora dye, a whale-song gramophone, a golden pickaxe and the title Legend of the Cove. None of the five is for sale at any price.

One mural, everybody's paws on it

A ninety-six by fifty-four wall stands in the plaza with fourteen colours and a twenty-second cooldown, so you get one pixel at a time. It never resets. Whatever the cove paints there stays painted.

Chat, friends and quiet words

Speech bubbles pop over your head, /w sends a private whisper, and clicking any capy opens their card and their personal bests. Add them as a friend and the list will take you straight to their den, or throw a party and see who turns up.

    Type a nickname, press the button, and the camera swoops down into Sunny Plaza. No account needed. Guest progress is kept on the device you played on.
